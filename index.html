The Massachusetts Computer Science Olympiad is a high-school programming competition hosted by the leaders of Brookline Computer Science Initiative. Our goal is to provide Massachusetts CS students with hands-on experience and opportunities to build a network of fellow coders through an in-person coding competition.

Questions we get a lot

Contact team@macso.org for any questions.

Q.Who can participate in the competition?

A.The competition is open to high school students of all programming backgrounds. Whether you are a beginner or an advanced coder, you are welcome to participate! After you get your Codeforces username verified, you can start the competition.

Q.How does the competition work?

A.The competition has two parts: an online qualification stage (Summer 2026) and in-person finals (separate online finals for those who can't make it). The qualification stage consists of three coding problems with no time limit (tie breakers decided from amount of time used), and the top 50 qualify for the finals.

Q.What is the scoring system like?

A.Qualification: IOI system. Each problem is graded either by groups or by each test individually. Finals: ICPC system. Each problem is either solved or not. The time penalty serves as tie breaker.

Q.When and where is the competition?

A.The competition will be held at the Brookline Teen Center Summer 2026. The in-person competition lasts three hours and includes free pizza, prizes (Amazon giftcards), and activities.

Q.How can I register for the competition?

A.You can register by clicking the Registration Form button at the top of the page, or directly through the registration form where you can fill in your details.

Q.Can I participate if I’m not in Massachusetts?

A.Yes, online participants who can’t make it in person can qualify for the online finals!
